Ratings are a starting point

NRCan ratings use standardized testing, not your actual driving. Traffic, temperature, speed, charging, loads and tyres affect use. Fuel consumption is weighted by your city/highway split. Battery-electric and charged plug-in modes use their published combined rating. Emissions and range are not predicted by this calculator.

Coverage includes conventional fuel vehicles and hybrids for 2015 onward, and battery-electric / plug-in hybrid vehicles from 2012. Earlier years in the selector contain electric catalogues only. Model variants are kept separate. No vehicle prices are supplied by NRCan.

Calculations stay in your browser. Saved comparisons include the selected source records and capture date; they are not silently replaced when the catalogue refreshes. The standard CoolCode visit counter receives only the tool ID.
